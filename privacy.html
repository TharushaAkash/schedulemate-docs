1. Introduction

Welcome to ScheduleMate ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to our mobile application and its related services.

2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

ScheduleMate requests access to your Google Account to provide core functionalities:

  • Google Drive Data (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive): Our app requires full access to your Google Drive. This access is strictly used to create study rooms (Google Drive folders), upload and share files within those rooms, and sync real-time chat messages via a hidden .chat.json file. We need this specific scope to allow you to join folders created by other users securely.
  • Basic Profile Information: We access your basic Google profile (Name, Email, Profile Picture) to display your identity inside the app and to your study room members.

3. Data Storage and Third-Party Sharing

We do not store your data on our servers. All files, timetables, and chat messages are stored directly in your personal Google Drive and the local storage of your device. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data or Google Drive data with any third parties under any circumstances.

4. Your Rights and Data Deletion

Since all data is stored in your personal Google Drive, you have full control over it at all times. You can delete any room, file, or chat history directly from the Google Drive interface or within the app. You can also revoke ScheduleMate's access to your Google account at any time through your Google Account Security settings.
